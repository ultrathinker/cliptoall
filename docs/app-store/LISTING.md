# App Store listing copy

Paste-ready. Character counts are given because App Store Connect enforces them
and silently truncates nothing — it just refuses to save.

The whole listing is built on the one property that is genuinely uncommon in
this category: **the screenshots go to storage the user owns.** Every competitor
of note uploads to their own service, which means an account, a quota, and a
link that dies when they do. That is the angle; the OCR is the second beat.

## Description

```
ClipToAll turns a screenshot into a shareable link in one press — and the link
points at storage you own, not at somebody else's server.

Press the shortcut, drag over the part of the screen that matters, and the
image is on your clipboard. Take one more step and it is uploaded to your own
Google Drive or your own Amazon S3 bucket, with the public link already copied,
ready to paste.

YOUR STORAGE, NOT OURS
Most screenshot tools upload to a service you have to sign up for, keep paying
for, and trust. ClipToAll uploads to an account you already have and already
control. There is no ClipToAll account, no quota, and no server of ours in the
path. If you stop using the app, your links keep working — they are your files.

READ THE TEXT IN ANY SCREENSHOT
Press Copy text and the words in the capture are on your clipboard, ready to
paste. Recognition runs entirely on your Mac using Apple's Vision framework, in
every language your Mac supports. Nothing is uploaded to read it, and it works
with the network switched off.

ANNOTATE FIRST
Arrows, boxes, freehand, text and blur — mark up what you mean before you send
it. Blur what should not be shared.

BUILT FOR A MAC
Lives in the menu bar, out of your Dock and out of your way. Captures at full
Retina resolution and tags the file so browsers show it crisp and correctly
sized instead of soft or twice too large — the difference is obvious the moment
someone opens your link on a Retina screen.

ALSO IN THE BOX
• A double press switches between copying the image and copying a link
• Reverse image search on Google or TinEye, one click from the result
• Save to a file, with a native save panel
• Themes, autoclose, a configurable shortcut
• Open source, Apache-2.0

ClipToAll asks for Screen Recording permission the first time you capture —
macOS requires it of any app that reads the screen. It collects nothing about
you.
```

## Things deliberately left out

- **Plugins.** Not in the macOS build at all, so not a word about them here.
  Claiming a feature the binary does not have is a rejection.
- **Multi-monitor.** Capture is primary-display only today. Do not imply
  otherwise; it is on the roadmap, not in the build.
- **Comparisons to named competitors.** Apple rejects listings that name other
  apps.
- **Any number we have not measured.** No "10× faster", no "used by thousands".
